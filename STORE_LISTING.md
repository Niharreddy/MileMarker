## Long description

Mile Marker is a private, offline-first trip tracker for the roads you
drive. Start recording manually, or let auto-detect notice when you're
driving and start tracking on its own — even with the app closed.

- **Automatic or manual trip recording**, with a live map of your route.
- **Photo pins** — attach a photo to the exact spot on your route,
  straight from the camera or your photo library. Mile Marker can also
  automatically match photos you took along the way, no manual tagging
  needed.
- **Comments anywhere along the route** — not just an end-of-trip review;
  drop a note at any point during the drive, timestamped and located.
- **Memories** — every photo across every trip, browsable in one place.
- **Export & import** — back up your trips or move them to a new device,
  as a single file you control. Nothing ever leaves your device unless you
  export it yourself.
- **No account, no ads, no tracking.** Mile Marker doesn't have a server —
  everything is stored locally on your phone.

Whether it's a cross-country road trip or your daily commute, Mile Marker
keeps a private, photo-and-note-rich record of the roads you've driven —
yours alone.
