A journal for the road, not just a tracker

Start recording manually, or let Mile Marker notice when you're driving and start on its own — even with the app closed.

🗺️

Automatic or manual trips

Hit start and go, or let auto-detect recognize driving speed and begin recording for you.

📍

Photo pins along the route

Attach a photo to the exact spot on your map — from your camera, your library, or matched automatically from photos you took along the way.

💬

Notes anywhere, not just at the end

Drop a timestamped, geotagged comment mid-drive so the little moments don't get lost in a single end-of-trip review.

🖼️

Memories, all in one place

Every photo across every trip, browsable in a single grid — a photo album organized by the road you took.

📤

Export & import

Back up your trips or move them to a new device as a single file you control — nothing leaves your phone unless you say so.

🔒

Private by design

No backend server, no account system. Mile Marker simply has nothing to collect.

Your trips stay yours

Mile Marker was built offline-first from day one. There's no server behind it — your routes, photos, and notes live only on your device.

  • Location is used only to record trips and place pins — never for anything else.
  • Photos you attach are copied on-device so they're never lost.
  • Nothing is transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly export it yourself.
